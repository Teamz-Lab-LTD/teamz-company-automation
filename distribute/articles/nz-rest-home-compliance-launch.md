Most facilities scramble in the weeks before, pulling records from 5 different systems.

## What It Does

It works **alongside your existing system** (VCare, or whatever you use). Not a replacement — a compliance evidence layer on top.

- **Record evidence in 60 seconds** — text, photo, or incident. 13 templates including Restraint Minimisation
- **AI structures your notes** — auto-detects which NZS 8134 standard the evidence maps to, flags risks
- **Compliance dashboard** — live readiness score across all 20 categories. See gaps before an auditor finds them
- **Audit packs** — one-click evidence export for certification audits. Professional, printable format
