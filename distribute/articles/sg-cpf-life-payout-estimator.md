CPF LIFE is Singapore's national annuity scheme — once you hit age 65, it pays a monthly payout for life, funded by your Retirement Account (RA) balance at 55. But most CPF members don't know there are two plans to choose from, and the right choice depends entirely on how you expect your expenses to evolve.

## Standard vs Escalating: The Core Difference

**CPF LIFE Standard Plan:**
- Higher initial monthly payout
- Payout stays approximately level for life (slight year-to-year variation)
- Lower bequest amount if you die early
- Better if you need maximum income now

**CPF LIFE Escalating Plan:**
- Lower initial monthly payout (about 20% less than Standard)
- Payout increases by approximately 2% per year
- Designed to keep pace with inflation over a 20-30 year retirement
- Better if you're in good health and expect a long retirement

**Example: RA balance $186,000 (Full Retirement Sum 2026) at 65**
- Standard: ~$1,570/month initially
- Escalating: ~$1,250/month initially → reaches ~$1,570 at approximately age 78

If you live past 78, Escalating pays more cumulative income. If you don't, Standard wins.

## What Determines Your Payout?

The monthly payout depends on:
1. Your RA balance when CPF LIFE starts (higher balance = higher payout)
2. Which plan you choose
3. The payout start age (65–70; deferring increases monthly amount)
4. Current interest rates and actuarial assumptions (CPF updates annually)

Voluntary top-ups to RA before 65 directly increase lifelong payouts — the Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS 2026: $426,000) sets the maximum top-up level.

## Voluntary Top-Up to Boost Payouts

Every dollar added to RA before 65 increases the lifelong monthly payout. At a 4% RA interest rate, a $10,000 top-up at age 55 grows to ~$14,800 by 65 and converts to roughly $80–$90/month for life — a powerful lever for those with spare cash or Ordinary Account funds to transfer.
