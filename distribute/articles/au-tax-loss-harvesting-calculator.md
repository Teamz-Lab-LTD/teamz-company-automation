# Australian Tax Loss Harvesting: How to Offset Capital Gains Before June 30

With the end of the financial year approaching on June 30, Australian investors face a critical question: should you sell underperforming investments to offset capital gains tax?

Tax loss harvesting — selling investments at a loss to reduce your CGT liability — is one of the most effective legal tax strategies available to Australian investors. Unlike the US, Australia has no "wash sale" rule (though the ATO's Part IVA anti-avoidance provisions apply if the sole purpose is tax avoidance).

## How It Works in Australia

1. **Identify unrealised losses** in your portfolio — shares, ETFs, crypto, or property
2. **Sell before June 30** to crystallise the loss in this financial year
3. **Offset against gains** — losses reduce your taxable capital gains dollar-for-dollar
4. **50% CGT discount** still applies to remaining gains held 12+ months
5. **Carry forward** any unused losses to future years (no expiry)

## The Numbers Matter

For an investor on the 34.5% marginal rate with $20,000 in capital gains and $8,000 in harvestable losses:

- Without harvesting: $3,450 in CGT (after 50% discount)
- With harvesting: $2,070 in CGT
- **Tax saved: $1,380**

## Key ATO Rules to Know

- Losses must be applied before the CGT discount
- You can't claim a loss on personal use assets under $10,000
- The ATO watches for "wash sales" where you rebuy the same asset immediately — while not explicitly prohibited like in the US, Part IVA can apply if the dominant purpose is tax avoidance
- Keep records of all transactions for at least 5 years
