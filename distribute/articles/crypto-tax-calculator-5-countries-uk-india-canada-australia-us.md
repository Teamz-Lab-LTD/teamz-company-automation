## The problem with generic crypto tax calculators

Most "crypto tax calculator" sites are US-only — or worse, they ignore country rules entirely and show one flat percentage. If you're in the UK and a generic tool tells you "28% capital gains" you're wrong (it's 18% basic / 24% higher after £3,000 allowance for 2026-27). If you're in India you get hit with a flat 30% + 4% cess + 1% TDS that no US-focused calculator models.

Instead of building one tool that lies, we built five country-specific tools that match the actual tax law.
