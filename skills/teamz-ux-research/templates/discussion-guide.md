## 0. Welcome + consent (2 min)

> "Thanks for joining. I'm [name] from [team]. Today we'll spend about
> 30 minutes looking at [product/feature]. There are no right or wrong
> answers — I'm learning from you. I'd love to record this so I don't
> have to take notes. Only my team sees it. You can stop at any time
> and still receive the incentive. Is that OK?"

Explicit verbal yes before proceeding.

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)

- "Tell me a little about yourself — what do you do, where are you based?"
- "When did you last [relevant behavior — e.g., watch a YouTube tutorial]?"

Goal: make them comfortable, get baseline context.

## 2. Context (5 min)

- "Walk me through the last time you [did the job]."
- "What were you using? What worked? What didn't?"
- "Take me back to the moment you [gave up / succeeded / moved on]."

Probe for: triggers, tools, friction, workarounds.

## 3. Task(s) (15 min)

Introduce think-aloud:

> "Once we get into [the product], I'd love it if you narrate your
> thoughts out loud. Don't explain — just say whatever's going through
> your head."

### Task 1 — [goal-framed task]

> "[Scenario context — e.g., 'You just installed this app and want X'].
> Take it from here."

Observe:
- Time on task
- Errors / wrong taps
- Verbalized confusion
- Recovery

Post-task:
- "How easy was that, 1 (very hard) to 7 (very easy)?"
- "What made it easy/hard?"

## 4. Wrap (5 min)

- "If you could change one thing about what we looked at, what?"
- "One word that sums up the experience?"
- "Would you open this tomorrow? Why/why not?"
- "Is there anything I should have asked that I didn't?"

## 5. Close (2 min)

> "That's everything. Incentive will arrive at [email] within [time].
> Thank you so much."
