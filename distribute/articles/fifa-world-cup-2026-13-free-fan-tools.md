## Why I Built These

The 2026 format is genuinely confusing. 48 teams instead of 32. 12 groups instead of 8. A new Round of 32 stage. 8 of 12 third-placed teams advancing — but which 8? FIFA published 495 possible Round of 32 combinations in Annex C of the regulations.

Most existing fan sites (FIFA.com, ESPN, BBC, Fox Sports) cover the **facts** — schedules, standings, qualified teams. What was missing: **interactive utilities** that answer personal fan questions — "if X wins their group, who do they play?", "when can Argentina meet France?", "which 8 third-place teams advance?", "which group is the Group of Death?".

All 13 interactive tools are built to fill those gaps. Each tool uses **real official FIFA data**: the December 2025 draw (groups), the April 2026 rankings, the full match schedule with venues and dates. Alongside them are 12 dedicated group guides (A–L), 16 host-city guides, and 20 country streaming guides.
