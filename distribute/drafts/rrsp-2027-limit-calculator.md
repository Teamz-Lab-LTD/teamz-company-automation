# RRSP 2027: CRA Confirms $35,390 Limit — Calculate Your Tax Savings by Province

The Canada Revenue Agency has confirmed the 2027 RRSP contribution limit at $35,390, up from $32,490 in 2026. With combined federal-provincial marginal rates reaching 53.53% in some provinces, the tax savings from maximizing your RRSP can exceed $18,000.

## Why the 2027 Limit Matters Now

RRSP contribution room is calculated as 18% of the previous year's earned income, capped at the annual maximum. For the 2027 tax year, that cap is $35,390 — meaning you need $196,611+ in 2026 earned income to hit the ceiling. Unused room carries forward indefinitely, so this year's planning affects next year's opportunity.

## RRSP vs TFSA vs FHSA in 2027

The RRSP isn't always the best choice. If your current tax rate is lower than your expected retirement rate, a TFSA might win. First-time home buyers should consider the FHSA ($8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime) which combines RRSP-like deductions with TFSA-like tax-free growth.
