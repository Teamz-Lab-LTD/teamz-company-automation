The new **Strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards (ACQS 2025)** rolled out in July, and most Australian providers are still catching up. If you manage an aged care facility, you already know the pressure: 7 new standards, 24 compliance categories, continuous improvement evidence, SIRS reporting — all while short-staffed.

I built a free tool to help: **[AlwaysReady Care](https://tool.teamzlab.com/apps/always-ready-care/au/)**.

## What It Does

It sits **on top of your existing care management system** (AlayaCare, Statura, MYP, or whatever you use). No rip-and-replace.

- **Record evidence in 60 seconds** — text, photo, or incident. 15 templates including SIRS Priority 1 and Priority 2
- **AI structures your notes** — auto-detects compliance categories, flags risks, suggests follow-up actions
- **Compliance dashboard** — live readiness score across all 24 ACQS categories. See gaps before an assessor finds them
- **Assessment packs** — one-click evidence export for quality assessments. Filtered by date, type, or standard
- **SIRS reporting templates** — Priority 1 (24-hour) and Priority 2 (30-day) incident templates with automatic classification
- **Restrictive Practices register** — track and document restraint use as required under Standard 4
