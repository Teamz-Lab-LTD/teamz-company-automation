Singapore's Budget 2026 includes three government-funded CPF top-up schemes worth up to **$5,200+ per eligible person**. Many Singaporeans don't check all three — and leave money unclaimed.

## The Three Schemes

**1. Matched Retirement Savings Scheme (MRSS)**
- Who: Singapore Citizens age 55–70, annual income ≤ $34,000, RA balance < FRS ($213,000 in 2026), AV of home ≤ $25,000
- What: Government matches your cash top-ups dollar-for-dollar, up to **$600/year**
- Where credited: Retirement Account (RA)

**2. Majulah Package (born 1960–1969)**
- **Earn & Save Bonus:** Up to $1,000/year credited to MediSave based on employment income
- **Retirement Savings Bonus:** $1,000–$1,500 one-off credit to RA depending on RA balance vs. BRS/FRS
- Who: Singapore Citizens born 1960–1969, income ≤ $34,000

**3. Silver Support Scheme (SSS)**
- Who: Singapore Citizens age 65+, income ≤ $23,000 over last 5 years, AV ≤ $13,000
- What: $240–$900/quarter (up to $3,600/year) credited to MediSave
