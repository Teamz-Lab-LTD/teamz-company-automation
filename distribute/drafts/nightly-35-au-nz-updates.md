Australian and New Zealand tax rates change every July. Three calculators just got updated to 2026-27 figures from ATO and IRD.

## HECS-HELP Repayment Calculator (Australia 2026-27)

The ATO updated HECS-HELP repayment thresholds for 2026-27. The minimum repayment threshold is now $54,435 (up from $51,550). There are 19 tiers, with rates from 1% at the threshold up to 10% for incomes above $159,664.

Key change: the indexation method switched from CPI to the lower of CPI or WPI after the 2024 student loan reforms. This means your balance grows slower — the calculator reflects the new indexation cap.

## Capital Gains Tax Calculator (Australia 2026-27)

Updated to the 2026-27 income tax brackets including the new 16% marginal rate for $18,201–$45,000 (down from 19%). The 50% CGT discount for assets held 12+ months remains. The calculator handles:

- Primary residence exemption
- 50% discount for individuals
- Net capital gain added to assessable income
- Medicare Levy 2% included

Source: ATO Capital Gains Tax schedule, Budget 2025-26 Stage 3 tax cuts.

## ACC Levy Calculator (New Zealand 2026-27)

ACC earner's levy updated to $1.67 per $100 of liable earnings (up from $1.60). New addition: Working Safer levy at $0.08 per $100 now shown separately. Maximum liable earnings capped at $142,283.

The calculator breaks down your total ACC cost including earner's levy, Working Safer levy, and shows the employer work levy component for reference.

Source: ACC.co.nz 2026-27 levy rates.
