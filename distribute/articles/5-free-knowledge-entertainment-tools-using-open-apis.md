Five tools for learning, discovery, and entertainment — all running in your browser, no login, no cost. Each one taps a free public API to give you live, real data.

## 1. Word Definition, Phonetics & Synonyms

[Look up any English word](https://tool.teamzlab.com/text/word-definition-lookup/) and get its full definition, phonetic pronunciation with audio playback, part-of-speech breakdown, example sentences, synonyms, and antonyms. Click any synonym to instantly look that word up too.

Includes a Word of the Day that rotates daily from a curated list of interesting vocabulary.

**Powered by:** Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev — no key, ~1000 req/hr)

## 2. Book Search & ISBN Lookup

[Search 20 million+ books](https://tool.teamzlab.com/tools/book-search/) by title, author, or keyword — or paste any ISBN-10/ISBN-13 to get full book details instantly. Shows cover images, publication year, subjects, and links to search on Amazon or Goodreads.

Hit "Random Classic" to discover a famous book from the archive.

**Powered by:** Open Library API (openlibrary.org — free, no key, unlimited)

## 3. Wikipedia Quick Facts Search

[Search Wikipedia without the ads and distractions](https://tool.teamzlab.com/tools/wikipedia-quick-facts/). Get clean article summaries with images in seconds. Switch between English, Spanish, French, German, and Arabic. Includes a "Today in Wikipedia" feed with featured articles for today's date.

**Powered by:** Wikipedia REST API (free, no key, CORS-enabled)

## 4. Trivia Quiz Generator

[Generate instant trivia quizzes](https://tool.teamzlab.com/evergreen/trivia-quiz/) on Science, History, Geography, Sports, Art, Animals, and 13 more categories. Choose easy/medium/hard difficulty and 5–20 questions. Includes a 15-second timer, live score tracking, letter grades, and high score memory per category.

Share your score: "I got 9/10 on History trivia — can you beat me?"

**Powered by:** Open Trivia Database (opentdb.com — free, no key, unlimited)

## 5. TV Show Episode Guide

[Search any TV show and browse all seasons and episodes](https://tool.teamzlab.com/tools/tv-show-guide/) with air dates, ratings, and episode summaries. Shows total episodes, average rating, and highlights the next upcoming episode for ongoing series.

Try: Breaking Bad, Stranger Things, The Office, Game of Thrones

**Powered by:** TVmaze API (free, no key, CORS-enabled)
