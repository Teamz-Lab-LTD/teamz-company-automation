Home warranty companies love to sell peace of mind. But does the math actually work in your favor? The average plan runs $300-$700/year plus $75-$125 per service call. A single HVAC replacement costs $5,000-$10,000. But how often does your HVAC actually break?

That's the calculation most homeowners get wrong — they compare the premium to a worst-case repair, not to the expected value of repairs over time.

## The Break-Even Math

A basic home warranty at $500/year with $100 service fees means you're paying $600-$800 annually before your first real repair is covered. Over 5 years, that's $3,000-$4,000. According to the Insurance Information Institute, the average homeowner spends $3,000-$5,000 on repairs per 5-year period — meaning a warranty barely breaks even for the average case.

Where warranties shine: older homes with aging HVAC, water heaters past their 8-10 year lifespan, or homes with original appliances from 15+ years ago. The expected repair cost in these cases far exceeds the premium.
