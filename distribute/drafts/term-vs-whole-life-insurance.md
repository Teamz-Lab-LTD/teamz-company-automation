# Term Life vs Whole Life Insurance: Which Costs Less Over Your Lifetime?

Life insurance is the financial product most people know they need but few understand well enough to buy confidently. The core decision: **term life** (cheap, temporary coverage) vs **whole life** (expensive, permanent coverage with cash value).

Our [Term vs Whole Life Insurance Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/insurance/term-vs-whole-life-comparison/) compares both options based on your actual age, health, and coverage needs — with a "buy term and invest the difference" analysis built in.

## What Makes This Calculator Different

Most insurance comparison tools just show premiums. Ours shows:

- **Premium comparison** — term vs whole life monthly and total costs
- **Cash value projection** — whole life's internal cash value growth at ~4%
- **Invest-the-difference analysis** — what happens if you buy cheaper term and invest the premium savings at 7% market average
- **Break-even age** — when whole life's cash value might justify the higher cost

## The Numbers Don't Lie

For a healthy 35-year-old male seeking $500,000 coverage:
- **20-year term**: ~$25/month ($6,000 total, $0 cash value at end)
- **Whole life**: ~$200/month ($48,000 by age 55, ~$35,000 cash value)
- **Buy term + invest difference**: $175/month invested at 7% = ~$91,000 by age 55

The math overwhelmingly favors "buy term and invest the difference" for most people. Whole life makes sense primarily for estate planning (especially with the OBBB-raised $15M estate exemption for 2026) or forced-savings discipline. Sources: [NAIC](https://www.naic.org), [III](https://www.iii.org).
