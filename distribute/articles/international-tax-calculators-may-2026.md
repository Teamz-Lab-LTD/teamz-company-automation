Cross-border professionals and small businesses face country-specific tax rules that English-language calculator sites usually overlook. Built five free 2026 tools this week, each rooted in official sources from Belastingdienst, NAV, CAE Luxembourg, Revenue.ie, and CRA. Everything runs in your browser.

## 1. NL WKR Werkkostenregeling 2026 (Netherlands)

Dutch employers got a 0.08 percentage point increase to the WKR vrije ruimte for 2026 — from 1.92% to 2.00% on the first €400,000 of fiscal wage, plus 1.18% above. Exceedances face 80% eindheffing.

The calculator includes the gerichte vrijstellingen (targeted exemptions like OV reimbursement and the €2.40/day work-from-home allowance), which don't consume the vrije ruimte. Critical for HR planning Christmas bonuses and team events.

**Try it:** [WKR Werkkostenregeling 2026](https://tool.teamzlab.com/nl/wkr-werkkostenregeling-employer-2026-calculator/)

## 2. NO Sykepenger Kalkulator 2026 (Norway)

Norwegian sykepenger pay 100% of salary up to 6G (estimated NOK 780,000 in 2026 after the May G-adjustment). The first 16 days are paid by the employer (arbeidsgiverperioden), then NAV takes over for up to 52 weeks.

The calculator separates the employer period from NAV period, handles self-employed and freelancer regimes, and shows the cost of NAV's tilleggsforsikring for self-employed who want 80%-100% coverage instead of the default.

**Try it:** [Sykepenger Kalkulator 2026](https://tool.teamzlab.com/no/sykepenger-2026-kalkulator/)

## 3. LU Congé Parental Luxembourg 2026

Luxembourg offers 4 regimes for parental leave: full-time 4 or 6 months, half-time 8 or 12 months, or fractional 20-month spread. The CAE pays a salary-proportional indemnity with 2026 plafond around €4,285/month and plancher around €2,057 (subject to CAE confirmation).

The calculator handles the four regimes, applies the plancher/plafond, and includes frontalier worker eligibility (CH, FR, BE, DE residents working in Luxembourg). Both parents can take leave independently — even simultaneously.

**Try it:** [Congé Parental Luxembourg 2026](https://tool.teamzlab.com/lu/conge-parental-luxembourg-rechner/)

## 4. IE CGT €1,270 Personal Exemption 2026 (Ireland)

Ireland's Capital Gains Tax exemption is €1,270 per person per year — non-transferable between spouses, non-cumulative. Standard CGT rate is 33%; offshore unit-linked funds and certain life assurance products attract 40%.

The calculator models gain calculation, loss set-off and indefinite carry-forward, and the two-tier payment cycle (December 15 for Jan-Nov disposals, January 31 for December disposals). Crypto is treated as a chargeable asset under standard 33% rules.

**Try it:** [Ireland CGT €1,270 Calculator 2026](https://tool.teamzlab.com/ie/cgt-personal-exemption-1270-2026-calculator/)

## 5. CA GST/HST Quick Method 2026 (Canada)

The Quick Method lets Canadian small businesses with under $400,000 in annual taxable supplies remit a flat percentage of GST/HST-included revenue instead of tracking individual ITCs. A 1% credit applies on the first $30,000 of supplies.

The calculator compares Quick Method vs regular method (after deducting estimated ITCs) across Ontario (HST 13%), Atlantic provinces (HST 15%), Quebec (GST 5%), and GST-only provinces. Service businesses with low input costs usually win — goods retailers usually don't.

**Try it:** [GST/HST Quick Method 2026](https://tool.teamzlab.com/ca/gst-hst-quick-method-2026-calculator/)
