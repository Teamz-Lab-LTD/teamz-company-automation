You inherited $50,000. Or sold a side business. Or finally built up enough savings to make a dent in your mortgage. Now the question: recast or refinance?

Most people default to refinancing because it's the option they've heard of. But mortgage recasting — where you make a lump-sum payment and your lender recalculates your monthly payment at the same rate — costs $150-$300 in fees vs $6,000-$15,000 for a refinance. No credit check. No appraisal. Done in 1-2 weeks.

## When Recasting Wins

Recasting beats refinancing when your current rate is already competitive. If you locked in at 3.5% in 2021 and today's rates are 6.5%, refinancing would actually increase your rate. A recast keeps your golden rate while lowering your payment.

The math: $300,000 balance at 3.5%, 25 years remaining. Recast with $50,000 lump sum → new payment drops $280/month. Recast fee: $250. Break-even: month 1. Total savings over remaining term: $84,000+.

Google Ads data shows "mortgage refinance calculator" gets 74,000 monthly searches at $4.76 CPC (DataForSEO, April 2026) — but most of those searchers would actually benefit more from a recast at today's rates.

## When Refinancing Wins

Refinancing wins when you can drop your rate by 0.75%+ and plan to stay in your home past the break-even point. The closing costs are higher, but the compounding interest savings over 15-30 years can dwarf a one-time recast.
