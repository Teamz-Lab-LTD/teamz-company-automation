Most first-time buyers don't realize they might qualify for a mortgage with literally zero down payment. Not FHA (3.5% down). Not conventional (3-5%). Zero.

The USDA Rural Development Guaranteed Loan program covers approximately 97% of U.S. land area — including many suburbs that don't feel "rural" at all. If your household income is under $110,650 (1-4 people) or $146,050 (5-8 people) in most counties, you could qualify.

## Why USDA Beats FHA on Cost

The numbers tell the story. USDA charges a 1% upfront guarantee fee and 0.35% annual fee. FHA charges 1.75% upfront MIP plus 0.55% annual MIP. On a $250,000 home, that difference saves you $500/year in annual fees alone — and you're financing $0 down payment vs $8,750 with FHA.
