## The 2026 Numbers

FHA loan limits for 2026 stand at $498,257 in most areas and $1,149,825 in high-cost metros (HUD, April 2026). The FHA annual MIP rate remains 0.55% for 30-year loans with LTV above 95%. On the conventional side, PMI rates from major insurers (MGIC, Genworth, Radian) range 0.30–1.50% depending on credit score and LTV — and automatically cancel once your loan balance hits 78% of the original appraised value.

The FHA mortgage insurance trap: if your down payment is less than 10%, MIP stays for the life of the loan. That means a $300,000 FHA loan at 5% down pays MIP for all 30 years — roughly $49,500 in total insurance premiums versus a conventional loan where PMI drops off after year 8-10.

## When FHA Wins

- Credit score 580–659 (FHA allows 580 minimum, conventional typically needs 620+)
- Down payment 3.5% (FHA minimum) with no PMI penalty for low scores
- DTI up to 50% (conventional usually caps at 43-45%)

## When Conventional Wins

- Credit score 700+ (lower PMI rates than FHA MIP)
- Down payment 10%+ (FHA MIP for 11 years vs PMI cancellation at 78% LTV)
- Any scenario where PMI cancellation saves more long-term than FHA's lower initial rate
