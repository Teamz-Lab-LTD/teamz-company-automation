# The UK Employment Rights Bill 2025 Changes Your Job. Here's a Free Checker That Shows How

The Employment Rights Bill, introduced in October 2024 and progressing through Parliament in 2025, is **the biggest change to UK employment law in a generation**. It scraps the 2-year qualifying period for unfair dismissal, restricts zero-hours contracts, ends "fire and rehire," and gives day-one rights to parental leave, sick pay, and flexible working.

## The Big-Ticket Changes

- **Unfair dismissal from day one** — currently you need 2 years of service. Under the Bill, you're protected from day one (subject to a statutory probation period, likely 9 months).
- **Zero-hours contracts** — workers will have the right to a contract reflecting their actual hours if worked regularly for 12 weeks.
- **Sick pay from day one** — no more 3-day waiting period. Eligibility threshold removed.
- **Flexible working as default** — employers must justify refusal, not the other way round.
- **Fire and rehire banned** — can only be used as a last resort with transparent consultation.
