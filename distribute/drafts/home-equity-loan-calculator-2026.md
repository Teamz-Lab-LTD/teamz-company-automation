## Tax Deductibility Under TCJA

Under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (still in effect through 2026), home equity loan interest is deductible **only** if the loan is used to "buy, build, or substantially improve" the home securing the loan. Using it for debt consolidation? No deduction. The IRS clarified this in [Publication 936](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936).

## How Much Can You Borrow?

Most lenders cap your combined loan-to-value (CLTV) at 80-85%. If your home is worth $400,000 and you owe $250,000, your available equity at 80% LTV is $70,000. The calculator shows this instantly and warns if you exceed safe thresholds.

Based on Federal Reserve H.15 data, average home equity loan rates sit around 8.5% as of April 2026. The calculator uses this as a default but lets you enter your actual quoted rate.
