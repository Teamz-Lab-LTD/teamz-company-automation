## 2. Wash Sale Rule Calculator (US Tax)

The IRS wash sale rule (IRC 1091) trips up a lot of investors during tax-loss harvesting season. If you sell a stock at a loss and buy it back within 30 days, your loss gets disallowed.

This calculator shows your disallowed loss amount, adjusted cost basis on replacement shares, and how the holding period carries over. Especially relevant now that wash sale rules apply to crypto starting 2025.

Source: IRS Publication 550.

**Try it:** https://tool.teamzlab.com/tax/wash-sale-rule-calculator/

## 3. Life Insurance Replacement Calculator

Thinking about switching life insurance policies? Most people underestimate surrender charges and the reset of the 2-year contestability period.

This calculator compares your current vs proposed policy — premiums, death benefit, cash value impact, and surrender cost. Based on NAIC Model Regulation 613 guidelines.

**Try it:** https://tool.teamzlab.com/insurance/life-insurance-replacement-calculator/

## 4. Division 296 Tax Calculator (Australia)

Australia's new Division 296 tax hits superannuation balances over $3 million starting July 2025. It's a 15% tax on earnings attributable to the portion above $3M — including unrealised gains.

This is the first free calculator for Div 296. Enter your opening and closing super balance, contributions, and withdrawals. It calculates the TSS earnings, proportion above $3M, and the additional tax.

Source: Australian Taxation Office, Treasury Laws Amendment (Better Targeted Superannuation Concessions) Act 2024.

**Try it:** https://tool.teamzlab.com/au/super-balance-tax-div296-calculator/

## 5. Hong Kong Stamp Duty Calculator

Hong Kong scrapped buyer's stamp duty (BSD) and special stamp duty (SSD) in February 2024. But the ad valorem stamp duty (AVD) at Scale 2 rates still applies to all property purchases.

This calculator uses the current AVD Scale 2 brackets (from HK$0 to 4.25% for properties over HK$21.739M). Enter your property price and it shows exactly what you'll pay.

Source: Inland Revenue Department, Hong Kong SAR.

**Try it:** https://tool.teamzlab.com/hk/stamp-duty-property-calculator/

## 6. Boligkøb Beregner (Denmark Home Purchase Calculator)

Buying a home in Denmark? The costs beyond the purchase price add up fast — tinglysningsafgift (registration fee), advokatsal (lawyer fees), bankgebyr (bank charges), and the mandatory udbetaling (5% down payment).

This calculator is in Danish and covers all the fees based on current 2026 rates from skat.dk and tinglysning.dk.
