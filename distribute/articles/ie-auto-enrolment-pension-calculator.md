# Ireland's Auto-Enrolment Pension Launches Soon — Here's a Free Calculator That Actually Explains It

Ireland's **My Future Fund** (auto-enrolment) starts phased rollout in 2025. By the time it's fully live, every employed worker aged 23-60 earning €20,000+ who isn't already in an occupational pension will be auto-enrolled.

The government is rolling this out because **65% of private-sector workers in Ireland have NO pension beyond the State Pension**. That's one of the worst retirement readiness figures in the EU.

## How Contributions Actually Work

Unlike a traditional occupational pension, auto-enrolment uses a **matching structure**:

- **Year 1-3:** You pay 1.5%, employer pays 1.5%, State tops up 0.5%
- **Year 4-6:** You 3%, employer 3%, State 1%
- **Year 7-9:** You 4.5%, employer 4.5%, State 1.5%
- **Year 10+:** You 6%, employer 6%, State 2% (total 14% of gross salary)

The State top-up replaces what would have been tax relief in a traditional pension — so higher-rate taxpayers get LESS from auto-enrolment than from a traditional AVC.

## The Big Question: Opt Out or Stay In?

You can opt out after 6 months, but you'll be re-enrolled every 2 years. The calculator runs both scenarios:

1. **Stay in:** Your pot at age 66 with compound growth
2. **Opt out:** What you keep in take-home pay today + invest yourself

Most workers come out ahead by staying in — but if you already max out an occupational pension or AVC, opting out to avoid dilution may make sense. The tool helps you decide.
