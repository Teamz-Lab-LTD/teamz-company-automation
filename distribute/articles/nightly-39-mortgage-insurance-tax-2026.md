## Mortgage cluster — the 2026 refi market

US mortgage rates have moderated from their 2024 peaks but most homeowners with 7%+ rates are still evaluating refinance options. We added four mortgage tools to handle the specific decisions that come up:

- **[Refinance vs Loan Modification Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/mortgage/refinance-vs-loan-modification-comparison/)** — compares a refinance against a Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac Flex Modification side-by-side. The Flex Modification has surpassed 88,000 completions per FHFA data and is the standard workout for borrowers in distress.
- **[2-1 Buydown Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/mortgage/mortgage-rate-buydown-2-1-calculator/)** — for buyers evaluating builder/seller-funded temporary buydowns. Year 1 saves 2 percentage points off the note rate, year 2 saves 1 point. We compute the seller-funded escrow amount required at closing.
- **[No Closing Cost Refinance Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/mortgage/no-closing-cost-refinance-calculator/)** — shows the true cost of "no closing cost" loans (lenders absorb costs via 0.25-0.50% rate premium). Includes break-even calculation for your specific holding period.
- **[Down Payment by State Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/mortgage/down-payment-by-state-calculator/)** — uses Zillow ZHVI and FHFA HPI data to show actual down payment requirements per state. Median home prices range from $170K (West Virginia) to $840K (Hawaii) — national averages mislead.

## Insurance cluster — gap-aware coverage planning

Insurance is the highest-CPC category in AdSense ($16-$28 per click for term life/auto), but the most underserved by quality calculators. Most online tools push policy quotes; ours run actuarial math that gives you the real number to ask for:

- **[Term Life Needs Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/insurance/term-life-needs-calculator/)** — uses both the DIME method (Debt + Income + Mortgage + Education) and the 10-12x income rule. Per LIMRA's 2024 Insurance Barometer, the average American is underinsured by ~$400,000.
- **[SR-22 Insurance Cost Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/insurance/sr22-insurance-cost-calculator/)** — for high-risk drivers (DUI, suspended license, at-fault accidents). Premium typically increases 70-180% depending on violation. The state filing fee itself is small ($15-$50); the cost is in the rate hit.
- **[Dental Insurance Cost Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/insurance/dental-insurance-cost-calculator/)** — compares insurance vs cash vs DSO discount plans using the standard 100/80/50 coverage rule. Per ADA data, adults who only need preventive care typically pay 30-50% MORE through insurance than they would paying cash.
- **[Critical Illness Insurance Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/insurance/critical-illness-insurance-calculator/)** — for closing the gap between health insurance OOP max ($9,450 ACA individual 2026) and your true financial exposure during cancer/heart attack/stroke recovery.

Two 2026-specific tax tools, both reflecting OBBB Act (P.L. 119-21, July 2025) changes:

- **[Short-Term Rental Tax Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/tax/short-term-rental-tax-calculator/)** — implements the famous "STR loophole" (avg stay ≤7 days + material participation = non-passive losses can offset W-2 wages).
