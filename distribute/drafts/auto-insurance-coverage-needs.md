State minimum auto insurance is a legal floor — not actual protection. In many states, the minimum bodily injury liability is just $25,000 per person. But the average bodily injury claim exceeds $20,000, and serious accidents regularly produce claims above $100,000 (NHTSA crash data, 2025).

If you cause a $200,000 accident with $25,000 coverage, you're personally on the hook for $175,000 — seized from savings, home equity, and future wages.

## The Deductible Trade-Off Most People Get Wrong

Raising your deductible from $500 to $1,000 typically saves 15-30% on collision and comprehensive premiums (iii.org). If you have $5,000+ in emergency savings, a $1,000 deductible makes mathematical sense — the premium savings compound over years of claim-free driving.

But if your savings are under $2,000, a lower deductible protects you from financial strain per-claim. The calculator factors your actual savings to recommend the right level.
