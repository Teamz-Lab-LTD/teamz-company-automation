# Super Tax Concessions 2026-27: Calculate How Much Tax You Actually Save

Australia's superannuation system offers some of the most generous tax concessions in the world — contributions taxed at 15% instead of your marginal rate (up to 47%). But with Division 293, Division 296, and the new better-targeted concessions regime taking effect, calculating your actual benefit has become genuinely complex.

## What Changed for 2026-27

The key changes affecting super tax concessions this financial year:

- **Concessional cap**: $30,000/year (unchanged)
- **Non-concessional cap**: $120,000/year (3-year bring-forward: $360,000)
- **Division 293**: Extra 15% on super contributions if income + super > $250,000
- **Division 296**: Additional 15% on *earnings* for balances above $3 million (effective from 1 July 2025)
- **Employer SG rate**: 12% (up from 11.5%)
- **LISTO**: Tax offset refunds the 15% for income ≤$37,000
- **Government co-contribution**: Up to $500 for income ≤$60,400
