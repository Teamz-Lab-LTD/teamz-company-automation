## Why Phone Resale Value Drops (And How to Prove Yours Hasn't)

Buyers on eBay, Swappa, Facebook Marketplace, and Craigslist discount phones for 3 main reasons:

1. **Unknown battery health** — a phone with 70% battery capacity is worth 30% less, but there's no way to verify without tools
2. **Possible root or unlock** — rooted phones can have security compromises; buyers can't tell from photos
3. **Unknown accident history** — drops, water damage, and repairs affect longevity

DeviceGPT solves all three with a single verified report.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the verification code truly unique to my device?**
A: Yes. It's generated from a cryptographic hash of your device's hardware identifiers combined with the scan timestamp. It cannot be generated on a different phone.

**Q: What if my battery health is low — should I still share the certificate?**
A: Transparency builds trust even when results aren't perfect. A buyer who sees honest data is less likely to dispute the sale or leave negative feedback. Price accordingly and be upfront.

**Q: Does the AI resale value match real market prices?**
A: It's an estimate based on health scores and device model — not a live market price. Use it as a reference alongside checking completed sales on eBay or Swappa for actual market rates.

**Q: Can buyers fake a certificate?**
A: No. The certificate is signed with a unique verification code tied to the device hardware. Screenshots from another phone will have a different code.
