Five regulators, five reports, one uncomfortable conclusion: most care home inspections arrive unannounced, and providers are judged on the evidence trail they already built — not the one they're about to scramble together when the auditor shows up.

Here's what the 2024–2025 data from CQC, ACQS, MD Bund, HIQA and HealthCERT actually shows. All numbers are sourced to the regulator's own publications.

## 🇬🇧 England — CQC State of Care 2024/25

Published 24 October 2025. Source: [cqc.org.uk/publications/major-report/state-care/2024-2025](https://www.cqc.org.uk/publications/major-report/state-care/2024-2025)

- **47%** of Requires Improvement services **fail to improve** on re-inspection
- **8%** get worse — drop to Inadequate
- Only **3.5%** of adult social care services reach Outstanding

The kicker: the services that fail re-inspection don't fail on care quality. They fail on Regulation 17 (Good Governance) — the evidence trail. Audits, records consistency, documented accountability. Not what your team does. What your team can prove.

## 🇦🇺 Australia — ACQS Sector Performance 2023–2025

Special edition report covering Apr–Jun 2025 plus two-year overview. Source: [agedcarequality.gov.au](https://www.agedcarequality.gov.au/news-publications/reports/sector-performance)

- **19%** of residential aged care providers were non-compliant in Q3 2024–25
- Home care compliance runs **~20 percentage points below** residential (≈39% non-compliant)
- Residential compliance rose from **58% (Q1 2022–23) to 81% (Q3 2024–25)** — +23 points in two years

The Royal Commission reforms pushed ACQS toward risk-based engagement. Yesterday's compliant provider may not clear today's assessment. Home-care operators are especially exposed.

## 🇩🇪 Germany — MD Bund 8. Pflege-Qualitätsbericht

Published 12 June 2025, data from 2023. Source: [md-bund.de](https://md-bund.de/themen/pflegequalitaet/pflege-qualitaetsberichte.html)

- **24,700+** quality inspections in a single year
- **167,000+** care-dependent residents directly assessed
- **1×/year unannounced** audits under the QPR framework (since October 2019)

Every German MD audit scores Mobility, Dekubitus, fall outcomes, weight loss and medication management at the resident level — not at the paperwork level. Gaps between your Pflegedokumentation and the resident's actual condition show up immediately.

## 🇮🇪 Ireland — HIQA 15 Years of Regulating Nursing Homes

Published October 2024 + 2024 inspection summary. Source: [hiqa.ie](https://www.hiqa.ie/sites/default/files/2024-10/15-Years-of-Regulating-Nursing-Homes-2009-2024.pdf)

- **840** HIQA inspections of nursing homes in 2024 alone
- **84% arrived unannounced**
- **13 non-compliance areas** are cited repeatedly year after year

The 13 gap areas HIQA keeps flagging: medication management, safeguarding, care plans, complaints, staff training, healthcare, risk management, records, residents' finances, fire precautions, infection control, premises, governance. If you haven't audited yourself against that list in the last 90 days, you're due.

## 🇳🇿 New Zealand — Ngā Paerewa NZS 8134:2021

In effect since 28 February 2022. Source: [health.govt.nz](https://www.health.govt.nz/regulation-legislation/certification-of-health-care-services/for-residents-and-families/rest-home-certification-and-audits)

- **4 Pae (quality domains)** assessed at every certification audit: Pae Ora, Pae Tū, Pae Ata, Pae Whakahaumaru
- **100%** of rest home audit summaries are **published publicly** on health.govt.nz
- **+** unannounced surveillance and spot audits between certifications

New Zealand's transparency model is unique — every Designated Auditing Agency report is searchable by whānau, competitors and local media. A single Partial Attainment stays on public record.

## What Actually Separates the Top-Rated Operators

The operators who stay in the top bands across every one of these jurisdictions share three habits:

1. **Capture evidence when the event happens**, not when inspection is due
2. **Run internal mock audits against the regulator's framework quarterly** (not annually)
3. **Treat the audit trail as a living document owned by every staff member**, not a folder owned by the manager
