# Your NZ Mortgage Rate Is About to Reset. Here's a Free Calculator That Shows the Damage

If you fixed your mortgage in 2021-2022 at 2.99%, your reset is coming. And the number you'll see on the OCR-indexed quote won't be pretty.

The median two-year fix in New Zealand landed at ~2.99% in late 2021. Today, the same two-year fix sits closer to 6.5-7%. For a $650,000 mortgage on a 30-year term, that's roughly **$1,350 more per month** — $16,200 per year of after-tax income, gone.

Most banks won't tell you the delta up front. Their "repayment change" letter is a single number with no context. So we built a free browser-based tool that gives you the full picture.

The **[NZ Mortgage Rate Rise Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/nz/mortgage-rate-rise-calculator/)** lets you:

---

## Why "Stress Test" Matters

The Reserve Bank of New Zealand recommends households model repayments at **2% above current rates** to sanity-check affordability. Most bank calculators skip this.

Ours doesn't. The stress-test row shows what happens if rates climb another 1%, 2%, or 3% — because if 2021-you got surprised once, 2026-you shouldn't.

---

## Fix vs Float — The Honest Take

Fixing locks certainty. Floating lets you pay down principal without break fees. The calculator shows side-by-side total interest over 2 years on both — you pick based on your cash flow, not a bank's preference.
