## The Power of Extra Payments

Adding just $50/month to a $15,000 loan at 8% over 5 years saves $462 in interest and pays it off 4 months early. The calculator shows this instantly — a feature most bank calculators hide because it reduces their revenue.

## Installment Loans vs Revolving Credit

| Feature | Installment Loan | Credit Card |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| Rate | Fixed (usually) | Variable |
| Payment | Predictable | Minimum varies |
| Term | Set end date | Open-ended |
| Credit impact | Diversifies mix | Utilization ratio |
