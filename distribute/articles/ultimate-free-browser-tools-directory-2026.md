I built 1,829 browser-based calculators, generators, and utilities organized across 98 categories spanning 30+ countries. Everything runs entirely in your browser — no backend servers, no sign-up, no data collection.

## Why Browser-Only?

- Client-side JavaScript execution — your data never leaves your device
- Offline functionality after initial load
- No account requirements or rate limiting
- Mobile-responsive with dark/light mode
- Auto-save using localStorage
