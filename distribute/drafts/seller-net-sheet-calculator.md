The sale price is not what you walk away with. Between agent commissions, closing costs, mortgage payoff, and taxes, sellers typically retain 88-93% of the sale price. On a $400,000 home, that gap is $28,000 to $48,000.

Most sellers don't learn this until closing day. This calculator shows you the real number upfront.

## What Gets Deducted (and How Much)

Post-NAR settlement (2024), the commission landscape shifted. Buyer's agent commissions are no longer automatically bundled. But sellers still commonly offer 2-2.5% to buyer's agents, plus 2.5-3% for their listing agent. On a $400K sale, that's $18,000-$22,000 in commissions alone.

Add title insurance (~$1,500-$3,000), transfer taxes (varies by state — $0 in some, $15,000+ in NYC), prorated property taxes, and any remaining mortgage balance. The calculator itemizes every deduction.

## Capital Gains — The Tax Most Sellers Forget

If you've owned your home less than 2 years or your profit exceeds $250,000 (single) or $500,000 (married filing jointly), you may owe capital gains tax on the sale. IRS Section 121 exclusion covers most primary residences, but investment properties, short-hold periods, and high-appreciation markets can trigger significant tax bills.

The calculator estimates your capital gains exposure based on purchase price, improvements, and holding period. Source: irs.gov Publication 523, cfpb.gov closing cost guidelines.
