# Capital Gains vs Ordinary Income Tax — The Holding Period Can Save You Thousands

Sell a stock after 11 months: taxed at up to 37%. Sell the same stock after 13 months: taxed at 0%, 15%, or 20%. The one-year holding period is the single biggest legal tax optimization available to individual investors.

Our [Capital Gains vs Ordinary Income Calculator](https://tool.teamzlab.com/tax/capital-gains-vs-ordinary-income-calculator/) shows exactly how much you save by holding investments longer than one year, including the 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT).

- **Total tax savings** from holding 1+ year — often $2,000–$15,000 per $50K gain

## 2026 OBBB Rate Stability

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21, signed July 4, 2025) extended or made permanent the TCJA tax brackets that were originally set to sunset December 31, 2026. The previously feared revert to 2017 rates — top bracket jumping from 37% to 39.6%, the 22% bracket becoming 25% — was largely averted. Long-term capital gains rates of 0%/15%/20% remain unchanged through OBBB. Source: [IRS.gov](https://www.irs.gov).

## The NIIT Factor Most People Forget

Investors earning above $200,000 (single) or $250,000 (married filing jointly) pay an additional 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax on capital gains — both short-term and long-term. On a $100,000 gain, that's an extra $3,800 in tax. The calculator auto-detects this based on your income and filing status, showing the true all-in tax rate.

## Real Example

A single filer with $120,000 salary sells stock for a $50,000 gain:
- **Short-term:** $50K taxed at 24% marginal = ~$12,000 tax
- **Long-term:** $50K taxed at 15% LTCG = ~$7,500 tax
- **Savings:** $4,500 just by waiting past the 1-year mark
