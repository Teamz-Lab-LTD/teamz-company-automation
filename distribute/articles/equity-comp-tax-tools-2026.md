## 1. ISO vs NSO Tax Comparison 2026

Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) avoid FICA (7.65%) on the exercise spread and convert the entire gain to long-term capital gains if you hold 2 years from grant + 1 year from exercise. Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs) tax the spread as ordinary income at exercise, always.

The catch: ISO exercise held across year-end triggers AMT on the bargain element at 26-28%. For employees granted both ISOs and NSOs, this calculator shows the after-tax dollar difference and whether qualifying disposition is worth the holding-period risk.

## 2. ESPP Qualifying Disposition Calculator

Section 423 ESPPs let you buy company stock at up to a 15% discount with a lookback feature — pricing off the lower of grant-date or purchase-date FMV. The qualifying-disposition rules require 2 years from offering date + 1 year from purchase date.

This calculator computes both outcomes side-by-side: qualifying (ordinary income capped at the lesser of plan discount or sale gain, rest as LTCG) versus disqualifying (full purchase-date spread as ordinary income). Most employees should sell same-day — but if you do hold, this shows the saving.

## 3. Nanny Tax Schedule H Calculator 2026

The 2026 household-employer threshold is $2,800 per worker in cash wages. Above that, you owe FICA (15.3% combined) plus FUTA (0.6%-6.0% depending on state credit-reduction status). Filing happens on Schedule H attached to your personal 1040 — not quarterly 941s.

The calculator handles the nanny-share case where employers gross up FICA on behalf of the worker, plus FUTA in credit-reduction states (California, New York, USVI for 2025 returns). Includes Dependent Care FSA vs Child and Dependent Care Credit comparison for the optimal benefit pairing.

## 4. Standard Mileage vs Actual Expense 2026

The 2026 IRS standard mileage rate is estimated at $0.70/mile for business. But the trap: if you use actual expense (Section 179, bonus depreciation, MACRS) in the first year a vehicle is placed in service, you're locked into actual expense for that vehicle's entire life. No switching back.

Calculator runs both methods on the same vehicle so you can see which wins — and warns about the first-year lock-in. OBBB extended 100% bonus depreciation through 2026, making actual expense more attractive on year-one purchases of heavy SUVs.

## 5. QBI Rental Safe Harbor Calculator 2026

Revenue Procedure 2019-38 created a safe harbor under which a rental enterprise qualifies for the §199A 20% QBI deduction — if you have 250+ rental service hours, separate books, contemporaneous logs, and file a signed statement.

The calculator validates all four requirements including the 3-of-5-year lookback for established enterprises (year 4+). Crucially, hours by your property manager count — favoring out-of-state and busy investors who delegate. Made permanent by OBBB.
